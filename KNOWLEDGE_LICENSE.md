# GammaThink published-knowledge licensing

Version 1.0 - 19 November 2026

## 1. Separate software and knowledge

GammaThink's implementation remains subject to the proprietary [LICENSE](LICENSE).
This document does not license that implementation, its original architecture
document, model weights or third-party source archives for general redistribution.

"Published Knowledge" means contributed knowledge material whose rights holder
or authorized representative has expressly identified it for publication under
this document. It may include symbolic Chain of Knowledge (CoK) nodes and links,
premises, rules, evidence annotations, derivations, corrections, technical
explanations, provenance and original selection or arrangement of knowledge.
It may be serialized as Knowledge DSL or incorporated into GKS snapshots.

Merely placing a file in this software repository does not designate it as
Published Knowledge. The designation must identify the material and the
applicable rights, for example in its contribution record or snapshot manifest.

Contributors retain ownership of rights they hold. The GammaThink maintainer
does not acquire exclusive ownership of contributors' knowledge by accepting,
merging or publishing it. Credit the actual authors and sources; do not
attribute another contributor's discoveries exclusively to the software author.

This document does not create copyright in facts, ideas, theories, mathematical
principles or other unprotected material. Uses that require no copyright
permission remain available without accepting additional copyright conditions.
No claim is made that wholly machine-generated material necessarily attracts
copyright.

It does not by itself authorize practicing a patented invention. Obtain any

## 4. Preserve third-party boundaries

A contributor can license only rights that contributor owns or is authorized
to grant. Do not label an entire archive, paper, dataset or model as CC BY-SA
merely because a graph references or quotes it.

Record third-party material, its source and its actual license separately.
Include protected expression only when permission, a compatible license or a
lawful exception permits the particular inclusion and downstream publication.
An exception permitting one use does not automatically grant a reusable public
license to the copied material.

A snapshot may contain separately identified materials with different lawful
licenses. Its publisher must preserve those distinctions, comply with
compatibility requirements and not claim that its manifest overrides them.
See [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## 5. No extra downstream software restrictions

The software license's prohibition on selling GammaThink does not prohibit
commercial reuse of separately licensed Published Knowledge. Its commercial
result-publication condition applies to persons using the Software under that
license, not to every person who only receives CC BY-SA knowledge.

CC BY-SA does not require publication of private adaptations, contributions
back to a particular repository or disclosure of every independent product
that uses a fact. Do not present those as conditions of the standard license.
The separate Software license may impose its own conditions on Software use.

## 6. Contributions, privacy and corrections

Publishing a contribution is voluntary. A local journal, private conversation
or unpublished reasoning graph is not automatically submitted or licensed by
using GammaThink. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before publication.

A copyright license is not consent to disclose personal data. Do not treat an
irrevocable copyright grant as a waiver of rights under Singapore's Personal
Data Protection Act 2012 or other applicable privacy laws.

Publishers may record disputes, corrections, invalidations and replacement
versions without silently rewriting the history of a scientific claim.
Content identity, logical verification and empirical truth remain distinct.
Acceptance or inclusion in a snapshot is not a guarantee that a claim is true.

No term here promises that a live global repository, a weekly publishing
service or an automated contribution-acceptance mechanism already exists.
The standard public license remains unmodified; Singapore law and forum terms
for the separate contribution agreement are set out in CONTRIBUTING.md.
