mutual consideration for this contribution agreement. Acceptance for review
does not promise acceptance into a graph, publication or a particular outcome.

necessary authorization from the actual rights holder. Where a person lacks
capacity, an appropriately authorized person with legal capacity must enter

- the submission and the rights being offered are accurately identified;

## 5. Public disclosure and personal data

Select the specific subgraph and supporting material to publish. Do not
automatically upload an entire local journal, private conversation history,
credentials or another person's confidential information. Prefer appropriately
anonymized knowledge records; do not assume replacing a name alone anonymizes
a record that can still identify someone.

Public distribution can include foreign hosting, downloads, caches and IPFS
peers. Before disclosure, the Publisher must establish the applicable lawful
basis and meet any Singapore PDPA notification, protection, retention and
overseas-transfer requirements. An acceptance of these copyright terms alone
does not establish all required permissions or transfer safeguards.

For any personal data intended for publication, separately identify the data,
purpose, intended public display and foreseeable overseas disclosure, and
obtain any required informed consent or establish another lawful basis.
Do not obtain an unlimited or irrevocable privacy waiver.

The Publisher must provide an appropriate privacy contact and handling
process before operating personal-data intake. Copyright acceptance records
must be protected and retained only as lawfully necessary. Do not post
identity documents or unnecessary private contact details in a public issue.

Withdrawal of personal-data consent, correction, access and other requests
must be handled under applicable law, including cessation of processing when

deletion of every independent cached or content-addressed copy. Explain the
actual consequences and take measures required for systems under the

## 6. Review, merging and snapshots

Before publishing, check the submission's license, provenance, schema,
dependencies, disclosed verification status and privacy implications. Keep
publication and acceptance into local reasoning distinct.

Corrections and invalidations should identify affected records and
dependencies. Preserve conflicting claims, their evidence and publication
history; merging is not a declaration of one globally accepted truth.

The Publisher may batch accepted records into immutable, indexed snapshots.
A weekly cadence is a possible operating policy, not a contractual guarantee.
Public manifests should identify included contributions, applicable licenses,
attribution sources, content identities and versions. Do not silently
relicense excluded third-party material during compilation.
