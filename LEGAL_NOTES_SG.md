# Singapore legal basis and remaining limits

Prepared 19 November 2026. This note explains the drafting choices; it is
not an additional condition on CC BY-SA 4.0 or a certification of
enforceability. The documents have not been reviewed by Singapore counsel.

## Copyright ownership and permission

IPOS distinguishes copyright ownership from a license and recommends
documenting the parties, rights, scope and obligations. Its guidance also
identifies the standard Creative Commons licenses as a route to permission:

- https://www.ipos.gov.sg/about-ip/copyright/introduction-copyright/
- https://www.ipos.gov.sg/about-ip/copyright/ownership-and-commercialisation/

Accordingly, the engine remains proprietary, while expressly designated
Published Knowledge receives a separate, unmodified CC BY-SA 4.0 license:

- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en

This does not create rights in unprotected facts or theories, assign
contributors' ownership, or guarantee copyright in machine-generated
material. It does not grant patent rights. Public technical disclosure
may affect patent protection; any intended patent strategy or patented
implementation needs separate assessment.

## Electronic agreement

Singapore's Electronic Transactions Act 2010, sections 6, 8 and 11,
recognizes electronic records and agreements and sets requirements for
methods used to identify a person and indicate intention:

- https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/ETA2010?ProvIds=pr6-,pr8-,pr11-

A document or checkbox alone does not establish contractual capacity,
rights-holder authority, informed assent, identity or consideration.
The contribution process therefore requires an identified submission,
affirmative authenticated acceptance, retained version/evidence and the
Publisher's express acceptance and undertaking to review. It does not
claim the current uploader implements these steps.

## Personal data and distributed publication

The Personal Data Protection Act 2012 addresses consent and other lawful
bases, withdrawal, reasonable purposes and overseas transfers, including
sections 13, 16, 18 and 26:

- https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/PDPA2012?ProvIds=pr13-,pr16-,pr18-,pr26-

A copyright license is not privacy consent. Consent cannot simply be
made irrevocable to match an irrevocable copyright grant. Public or IPFS
distribution does not exempt the operator from applicable duties.
Before live intake, establish the operator's identity, privacy/DPO
contact, notices, lawful processing and transfer basis, security,
retention and withdrawal/correction procedures. Determine which duties
and exceptions apply to the actual operation; do not assume public
availability alone resolves them.

## Liability and courts

The proprietary license and contribution agreement select Singapore law
and non-exclusive Singapore courts, subject to mandatory protections.
Their liability language preserves negligence death/personal-injury
liability, fraud and other non-excludable rights.

The Unfair Contract Terms Act 1977 contains negligence and reasonableness
rules, but its scope includes business-liability limits and First
Schedule exceptions for contracts so far as they concern creation,
transfer or termination of intellectual-property rights. Do not claim
that every provision automatically applies to every copyright license:

- https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/UCTA1977
- https://sso.agc.gov.sg/Act/UCTA1977?ProvIds=Sc1-

No disclaimer establishes that every exclusion will be upheld. Applicable
consumer law, jurisdiction rules and the circumstances of contracting
still matter.

## Matters not cured by these documents

- Earlier valid irrevocable project licenses remain effective for the
  copies and rights they covered.
- The full build's GPL-licensed libzim dependency remains a separate
  compatibility issue for proprietary combined-binary distribution.
- The identity, capacity and actual ownership/authority of the Licensor,
  Publisher and contributors must be established when needed; a public
  account name is not proof of the complete chain of title.
- Third-party evidence, archives, weights, personal data and contributed
  material cannot be relicensed merely by including them in a graph.
- Publishing and legal intake controls require an actual process. These
  files do not deploy a global service, implement consent collection or
  authorize automatic publication of private journals.

The original full-publication condition for commercial results remains a
separate condition on Software use. It is not added as a restriction to
the standard public knowledge license. Singapore legal review is still
needed for that bespoke condition and the actual planned distribution
and contribution service.
